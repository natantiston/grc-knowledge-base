# 17.15 Security Awareness Tools and Templates

## Part 1 – Security Awareness Training Plan

A Security Awareness Training Plan defines how an organization educates employees, contractors, and other relevant personnel about cybersecurity responsibilities, security policies, threats, safe behavior, and regulatory or organizational requirements.

The plan should be risk-based and aligned with the organization's security objectives. It should identify who needs training, what they need to know, how training will be delivered, how completion will be monitored, and how effectiveness will be measured.

```text
New Employee Requirement:

All new employees must complete

```

For example:

```text
Privileged Users:

Security training must be completed

```

The plan should define **refresher training**.

For example:

```text
Refresher Training:

Annual security awareness training
is mandatory for all employees.

Additional refresher training may be
assigned following significant incidents,
policy changes, or identified knowledge
gaps.
```

The organization should establish a **training completion target**.

For example:

```text
Training Completion Target:

≥ 98% within the required training period
```

The target should be measurable and approved by the appropriate management authority.

The plan should define the **assessment method**.


For example:

```text
Assessment Method:

Employees must achieve at least 80%
on the post-training knowledge assessment.

Employees failing the assessment must
complete remedial training.
```

The plan should define how **training effectiveness** will be measured.

Training completion alone does not demonstrate that employees have become more security-aware.

Effectiveness may be measured using:

```text
Training Completion Rate

Assessment Scores

Phishing Failure Rate

Phishing Reporting Rate

Repeat Phishing Failure Rate

Security Incident Trends

Policy Violation Trends

Security Reporting Behavior

Employee Feedback

Knowledge Retention
```

For example:

```text
Effectiveness Target:

Reduce phishing failure rate from 12%
to below 5% within 12 months.
```

The organization should monitor the **training completion rate**.

For example:

```text

4,410

98%
```

Security awareness training should be connected to the organization's broader **risk management process**.

For example:

```text
Security Risk
      ↓
Human-Related Risk Assessment
      ↓
Security Awareness Requirement
      ↓
Training Program
      ↓
Assessment
      ↓
Behavioral Measurement
      ↓
Risk Evaluation
      ↓
Improvement Actions
```

This ensures that security awareness is treated as a risk management activity rather than simply a compliance requirement.


The organization should evaluate whether awareness training is producing measurable behavioral improvements.

For example:

```text
Before Training:

Phishing Click Rate:
12%

After Training:

Phishing Click Rate:
7%

4%
```

This provides stronger evidence of effectiveness than training completion alone.

Another example:

```text
Before Awareness Campaign:

Phishing Reporting Rate:
38%

After Awareness Campaign:

Phishing Reporting Rate:
71%
```

The GRC professional should compare awareness metrics against relevant security incidents.

For example:

```text
Security Awareness Result:

Phishing failure rate decreased.

Security Incident Result:

Credential-related incidents also decreased.

Conclusion:

Awareness activities may be contributing
to reduced human-related security risk.
```

The GRC professional should avoid claiming causation unless sufficient evidence exists.

```text
Risk Assessment
      ↓
Identify Human-Related Risks
      ↓

      ↓
Develop Training Content
      ↓
Deliver Training
      ↓
Assess Knowledge
      ↓

      ↓
Analyze Results
      ↓
Improve Training Program
```

The key principle is:

> **A security awareness training plan should provide a structured, risk-based approach for educating personnel, measuring security knowledge and behavior, addressing identified weaknesses, and continuously improving the organization's human security posture.**
