# 20.25 Final GRC Knowledge Model

This section presents the **final integrated GRC knowledge model** for the entire GRC Knowledge Base, bringing together the concepts developed from **Chapter 1 through Chapter 20**.

The purpose of this model is to provide a single mental framework that a GRC professional can use to understand, design, implement, assess, operate, and continuously improve an enterprise GRC program.

---

# 20.25.1 The Purpose of the GRC Knowledge Model

GRC is not simply a collection of:

* policies;
* frameworks;
* controls;
* audits;
* risk registers;
* compliance requirements;
* cybersecurity tools;
* reports.

A mature GRC capability connects these elements to **business strategy and decision-making**.

The fundamental relationship is:

**Business Strategy**

→ **Objectives**

→ **Risk**

→ **Governance**

→ **Requirements**

→ **Controls**

→ **Assurance**

→ **Monitoring**

→ **Decision**

→ **Improvement**

The GRC function exists to ensure that these relationships remain connected.

---

# 20.25.11 The Unified Control Concept

Multiple frameworks may require similar controls.

For example:

**ISO 27001**

requires access control.

**NIST**

requires access management.

**PCI DSS**

requires access restrictions.

**GDPR**

requires appropriate security measures.

**NIS2**

requires cybersecurity risk-management measures.

Rather than creating five completely separate controls, the organization can establish a common enterprise control.

Example:

> **Enterprise Identity and Access Management Control**

This control can satisfy multiple requirements.

This is the foundation of a **Common Control Framework (CCF)** or **Unified Control Framework (UCF)**.

# 20.25.37 Final GRC Knowledge Statement

The complete GRC Knowledge Base can be summarized in one sentence:

> **GRC is the integrated discipline of governing the organization, understanding and managing risk, meeting applicable obligations, implementing effective controls, obtaining assurance, maintaining resilience, and providing decision-makers with the information required to achieve strategic objectives responsibly.**

And the ultimate progression is:

**Govern**

→ **Understand**

→ **Assess**

→ **Control**

→ **Assure**

→ **Monitor**

→ **Decide**

→ **Improve**

→ **Govern Again**

This completes **Chapter 20 — GRC Glossary**, and with it the conceptual framework of the **GRC Knowledge Base from Chapter 1 through Chapter 20**.
