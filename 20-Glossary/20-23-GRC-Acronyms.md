# 20.23 GRC Acronyms

This section provides a consolidated reference of the **most important acronyms used across governance, risk, compliance, cybersecurity, information security, privacy, business continuity, AI governance, audit, third-party risk, cloud security, and related GRC disciplines**.

The objective is to provide a practical reference for GRC professionals who need to understand terminology used in **standards, regulations, audits, risk assessments, executive reporting, security programs, consulting engagements, and certification examinations**.
