# 20.14 Privacy and Data Protection Terms

This section defines the core terminology associated with **Privacy, Data Protection, Personal Data, GDPR, Privacy Governance, Data Protection Impact Assessments, Data Subject Rights, Privacy Risk Management, Data Governance, and Privacy by Design**.

> **Terminology note:** This glossary is designed for the GRC knowledge base and uses internationally common privacy terminology, with particular emphasis on **GDPR and EU data-protection concepts**. Where a term has a specific legal meaning under a particular jurisdiction, that jurisdiction should take precedence.

---

# Privacy and Data Protection Glossary

| Term                                             | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Privacy**                                      | The protection of individuals' ability to control or have appropriate safeguards over information relating to them and how it is collected, used, shared, and retained.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data Protection**                              | The legal, organizational, and technical measures used to protect personal data and the rights of individuals.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Privacy Governance**                           | The governance structures, policies, roles, processes, and controls used to manage privacy obligations and risks.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Management**                           | The processes used to identify, assess, manage, monitor, and improve privacy practices.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Privacy Program**                              | The organizational framework for managing privacy requirements, risks, controls, compliance, and data-subject rights.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Data Protection Program**                      | Structured program for implementing and maintaining data-protection requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Policy**                               | Statement explaining how an organization collects, uses, shares, stores, protects, and manages personal information.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Protection Policy**                       | Organizational policy defining requirements for protecting personal data and complying with applicable data-protection laws.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Privacy Notice**                               | Information provided to individuals explaining how their personal data is processed and what rights they have.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Privacy Statement**                            | Public or internal statement describing an organization's privacy practices.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Personal Data**                                | Information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**    | Information that can identify or potentially identify an individual. The exact legal definition varies by jurisdiction.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data Subject**                                 | An identified or identifiable natural person to whom personal data relates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Individual Rights**                            | Legal rights individuals may have regarding their personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Data Controller**                              | Under GDPR, the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Controller**                                   | Short form for data controller.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Joint Controllers**                            | Two or more entities that jointly determine the purposes and means of processing personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Data Processor**                               | Under GDPR, an entity that processes personal data on behalf of a controller.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Processor**                                    | Short form for data processor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Subprocessor**                                 | A processor engaged by another processor to perform processing activities on behalf of the controller.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Third-Party Processor**                        | External organization processing personal data under contractual or other authorized arrangements.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Recipient**                                    | Person, organization, public authority, agency, or other entity to which personal data is disclosed.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**                | Designated person responsible for specified privacy and data-protection advisory, monitoring, and coordination functions under applicable law.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Privacy Officer**                              | Person responsible for managing or coordinating an organization's privacy program.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Chief Privacy Officer (CPO)**                  | Senior executive responsible for organizational privacy strategy, governance, and oversight.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Privacy Champion**                             | Employee who promotes privacy awareness and supports privacy practices within a business unit.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Supervisory Authority (SA)**                   | Independent public authority responsible for monitoring and enforcing applicable data-protection laws.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Lead Supervisory Authority**                   | Supervisory authority primarily responsible for certain cross-border processing activities under GDPR's one-stop-shop mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Data Protection Authority (DPA)**              | Common term for a governmental or regulatory authority responsible for enforcing data-protection laws.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)**     | Structured assessment used to identify and mitigate privacy risks associated with processing personal data, particularly processing likely to result in high risk to individuals.                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)**              | Assessment of privacy impacts and risks associated with a project, system, product, service, or processing activity.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Privacy Risk Assessment**                      | Process for identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating risks to individuals arising from personal-data processing.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Privacy Risk**                                 | Risk that processing personal data may adversely affect individuals, their rights, freedoms, expectations, or interests.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Data Protection Risk**                         | Risk associated with failure to adequately protect personal data or comply with data-protection requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Privacy by Design**                            | Principle of incorporating privacy considerations into the design and architecture of systems, products, services, and processes from the beginning.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Privacy by Default**                           | Principle that appropriate privacy-protective settings and practices should be applied by default without requiring unnecessary action from individuals.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Data Protection by Design and by Default**     | GDPR principle requiring appropriate technical and organizational measures to integrate data protection into processing activities and default configurations.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Data Minimization**                            | Principle that personal data collected and processed should be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the stated purpose.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Purpose Limitation**                           | Principle that personal data should be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and not processed incompatibly with those purposes.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Storage Limitation**                           | Principle that personal data should not be kept longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable requirements.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Accuracy Principle**                           | Requirement that personal data be accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Integrity and Confidentiality**                | Requirement to protect personal data against unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction, or damage.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Accountability Principle**                     | Requirement for organizations to be responsible for compliance with applicable data-protection principles and able to demonstrate that compliance.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**       | GDPR principle requiring processing to have a lawful basis, be fair to individuals, and be communicated transparently.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Lawful Processing**                            | Processing carried out on a legally recognized basis.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Legal Basis**                                  | Legitimate legal justification for processing personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Consent**                                      | Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of an individual's wishes permitting processing of personal data where consent is the applicable legal basis.                                                                                                                              |
| **Explicit Consent**                             | Clearly expressed consent generally requiring a stronger affirmative indication and used where law requires a higher level of consent.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Informed Consent**                             | Consent provided after the individual receives sufficient information about the processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Consent Management**                           | Processes and technologies for collecting, recording, managing, updating, and withdrawing consent.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Consent Withdrawal**                           | Individual's exercise of the right to withdraw previously provided consent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Contractual Necessity**                        | Legal basis allowing processing where it is necessary to perform or enter into a contract with the data subject.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Legal Obligation**                             | Legal basis allowing processing necessary to comply with a legal obligation applicable to the controller.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Vital Interests**                              | Legal basis permitting processing necessary to protect the vital interests of an individual or another person in defined circumstances.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Public Task**                                  | Legal basis permitting processing necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or official authority.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Legitimate Interests**                         | Legal basis allowing processing where the controller or third party has legitimate interests that are not overridden by the individual's interests, rights, or freedoms.                                                                                                                                |
| **Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)**         | Assessment used to determine whether processing based on legitimate interests is appropriate and proportionate.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Processing**                                   | Any operation performed on personal data, such as collection, recording, organization, storage, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, restriction, or deletion.                                                                                                                         |
| **Processing Activity**                          | Specific activity or set of activities involving personal-data processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Processing Purpose**                           | Defined reason or objective for processing personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Processing Operation**                         | Specific action performed on personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Automated Processing**                         | Processing performed through automated means without requiring manual handling of each data item.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Profiling**                                    | Automated processing of personal data used to evaluate or predict aspects relating to an individual, such as preferences, behavior, performance, or interests.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Automated Decision-Making**                    | Decision-making performed by automated means, potentially including decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Human Oversight**                              | Human involvement in automated or AI-supported decision-making intended to provide review, intervention, challenge, or accountability.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Special Categories of Personal Data**          | GDPR category of particularly sensitive personal data, including information concerning racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic data, biometric data used for identification, health data, and sex life or sexual orientation. |
| **Sensitive Personal Data**                      | General term used for personal information requiring enhanced protection due to its sensitivity; the precise legal definition varies by jurisdiction.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Criminal Offence Data**                        | Personal data concerning criminal convictions and offences or related security measures subject to specific legal requirements.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Genetic Data**                                 | Personal data relating to inherited or acquired genetic characteristics that provide unique information about an individual's physiology or health.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Biometric Data**                               | Personal data resulting from specific technical processing relating to physical, physiological, or behavioral characteristics that can enable or confirm unique identification.                                                                                                                         |
| **Health Data**                                  | Personal data related to an individual's physical or mental health, including healthcare services.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Pseudonymization**                             | Processing personal data so it can no longer be attributed to a specific individual without additional information kept separately and protected.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Anonymization**                                | Processing data so that individuals are no longer identifiable, considering reasonably available means of identification.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Anonymous Data**                               | Data that does not relate to an identified or identifiable individual.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **De-identification**                            | Techniques used to reduce or remove direct identifiers or other information that can identify individuals.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Re-identification**                            | Process of linking de-identified or pseudonymized data back to an identifiable individual.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Data Masking**                                 | Technique that obscures sensitive information while preserving enough information for authorized use.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Data Redaction**                               | Removal or concealment of sensitive information from a document or dataset.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Tokenization**                                 | Replacement of sensitive data with tokens that have no direct meaning outside a controlled mapping system.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Data Aggregation**                             | Combining multiple records or data points into summarized information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Collection**                              | Acquisition or gathering of personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Data Creation**                                | Generation of new personal data through business processes, systems, analytics, or other activities.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Use**                                     | Application or consumption of personal data for an authorized purpose.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Sharing**                                 | Providing personal data to another organization, system, individual, or business function.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Data Disclosure**                              | Making personal data available or communicating it to another party.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Transfer**                                | Movement of personal data between systems, organizations, locations, or jurisdictions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **International Data Transfer**                  | Transfer of personal data from one country or jurisdiction to another.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Cross-Border Data Transfer**                   | Transfer of personal data across national or jurisdictional boundaries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Third-Country Transfer**                       | Under EU data-protection terminology, transfer of personal data to a country outside the relevant European Economic Area framework.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Adequacy Decision**                            | Formal determination that a jurisdiction provides an adequate level of personal-data protection for purposes of international transfers under GDPR.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**          | Standard contractual mechanisms approved by the European Commission for certain international transfers of personal data.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)**               | Internal data-protection rules used by multinational groups to govern certain international transfers of personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)**             | Assessment of legal, technical, and organizational risks associated with transferring personal data to another jurisdiction.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**              | Contract defining the rights, obligations, responsibilities, and processing requirements between a controller and processor.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)**               | Contractual document, often attached to a commercial agreement, addressing data-processing requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Controller-Processor Agreement**               | Contract establishing requirements governing processing performed by a processor for a controller.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Subprocessor Agreement**                       | Agreement governing the use of subprocessors and their data-protection responsibilities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Data Sharing Agreement**                       | Agreement defining how personal data will be shared, used, protected, and governed between parties.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Joint Controller Agreement**                   | Arrangement defining responsibilities between entities that jointly determine purposes and means of processing.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Records of Processing Activities (ROPA)**      | Documented records describing an organization's processing activities, purposes, categories of data, recipients, retention, transfers, and safeguards.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Record of Processing**                         | Individual documented record describing a processing activity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Data Inventory**                               | Structured inventory of data assets, data types, owners, locations, systems, and processing activities.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Personal Data Inventory**                      | Inventory identifying personal data processed by an organization and relevant processing characteristics.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Data Flow Mapping**                            | Documentation showing how personal data moves between systems, processes, organizations, countries, and third parties.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Flow Diagram (DFD)**                      | Visual representation of data movement between systems, processes, users, and other entities.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Data Lineage**                                 | Documentation of the origin, transformation, movement, use, and destination of data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Owner**                                   | Person accountable for the appropriate governance and use of a particular data asset or dataset.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Data Steward**                                 | Person responsible for operational management, quality, classification, and governance of data.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Privacy Owner**                                | Person accountable for privacy requirements associated with a process, product, system, or business activity.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Data Custodian**                               | Person or function responsible for technical management and protection of data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Data Lifecycle**                               | Stages through which data passes from creation or collection through use, storage, retention, archival, and deletion.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Data Retention**                               | Period during which personal data is maintained for legitimate business, legal, regulatory, or contractual purposes.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Retention Period**                             | Defined length of time personal data should be retained.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Retention Schedule**                           | Document defining retention requirements for different categories of information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Data Deletion**                                | Removal of personal data when it is no longer required or when deletion is otherwise legally required.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Erasure**                                 | Removal of personal data from systems or records.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Right to Erasure**                             | Data subject right under GDPR to request deletion of personal data in specified circumstances.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Right to Be Forgotten**                        | Common term for the GDPR right to erasure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Secure Disposal**                              | Controlled process for securely destroying or deleting data so that unauthorized recovery is prevented or appropriately minimized.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Data Destruction**                             | Physical or logical destruction of data or storage media.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Data Subject Access Request (DSAR)**           | Request from an individual seeking access to personal data held or processed about them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Subject Access Request (SAR)**                 | Common alternative term for a data subject access request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Right of Access**                              | Right of an individual to obtain confirmation and access to personal data and certain related information.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Right to Rectification**                       | Right to have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Right to Erasure**                             | Right to request deletion of personal data in circumstances defined by applicable law.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Right to Restriction of Processing**           | Right to request limitation of how personal data is processed in specified circumstances.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Right to Data Portability**                    | Right to receive certain personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and potentially transmit it to another controller.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Right to Object**                              | Right to object to certain types of processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and certain direct marketing activities.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Right to Withdraw Consent**                    | Right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Rights Request Management**                    | Processes for receiving, authenticating, assessing, fulfilling, documenting, and closing data-subject rights requests.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Subject Request Verification**            | Process of verifying the identity of an individual submitting a rights request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Data Subject Request Fulfillment**             | Process of completing and responding to a valid individual rights request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Privacy Complaint**                            | Complaint concerning an organization's collection, use, disclosure, protection, or management of personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Data Protection Complaint**                    | Formal complaint relating to possible violations of data-protection requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Personal Data Breach**                         | Under GDPR, a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to personal data.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Privacy Breach**                               | General term describing an incident involving unauthorized access, use, disclosure, loss, or compromise of personal information.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Data Breach**                                  | Security incident involving unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction, or compromise of data.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Breach Detection**                             | Identification of a suspected or confirmed data-protection breach.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Breach Assessment**                            | Evaluation of the nature, scope, consequences, affected individuals, and risks associated with a personal-data breach.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Breach Notification**                          | Communication of a personal-data breach to regulators, affected individuals, customers, or other required parties.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Supervisory Authority Notification**           | Notification of a qualifying personal-data breach to the relevant supervisory authority where legally required.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Data Subject Breach Notification**             | Communication to affected individuals regarding a qualifying personal-data breach where required by law.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Breach Response Plan**                         | Documented procedures for detecting, assessing, containing, investigating, notifying, and recovering from personal-data breaches.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Incident**                             | Event that may compromise privacy, personal data, data-subject rights, or privacy obligations.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Privacy Incident Response**                    | Processes used to detect, investigate, contain, resolve, and learn from privacy incidents.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Privacy Incident Register**                    | Record of privacy incidents, investigations, actions, notifications, and outcomes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Risk to Rights and Freedoms**                  | Potential impact of personal-data processing on individuals' rights, freedoms, interests, or dignity.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **High-Risk Processing**                         | Processing likely to result in a high risk to individuals and therefore potentially requiring enhanced assessment or safeguards.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Large-Scale Processing**                       | Processing involving significant quantities of personal data, numbers of individuals, geographical scope, duration, or other relevant factors.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Systematic Monitoring**                        | Repeated, organized, or methodical monitoring of individuals, particularly where required by applicable privacy law.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **High-Risk Processing Activity**                | Processing activity presenting elevated privacy risks because of scale, sensitivity, technology, profiling, monitoring, or other characteristics.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Threshold Assessment (PTA)**           | Preliminary assessment used to determine whether a more detailed privacy impact assessment is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data Protection Impact Assessment Register**   | Record of DPIAs performed, their status, findings, approvals, and mitigation actions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **DPIA Screening**                               | Initial process for determining whether a proposed processing activity requires a DPIA.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **DPIA Mitigation**                              | Technical, organizational, legal, or procedural measures implemented to reduce privacy risks identified through a DPIA.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Residual Privacy Risk**                        | Privacy risk remaining after safeguards and risk treatments have been implemented.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Privacy Risk Acceptance**                      | Formal decision to accept a defined residual privacy risk.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Privacy Control**                              | Technical, organizational, contractual, or procedural measure designed to manage privacy risk or meet privacy requirements.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Privacy Control Framework**                    | Structured set of privacy controls mapped to legal, regulatory, policy, and business requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)** | Security and organizational measures implemented to protect personal data and support compliance.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Appropriate Security Measures**                | Security controls proportionate to the risks associated with processing personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Protection Safeguards**                   | Technical, organizational, contractual, and procedural measures designed to protect personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Pseudonymization Control**                     | Control using pseudonymization to reduce identification risk while preserving data utility.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Encryption Control**                           | Control protecting personal data through encryption and appropriate cryptographic key management.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Access Control**                               | Controls restricting personal-data access to authorized individuals, systems, and processes.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Data Segregation**                             | Separation of personal data based on business, security, privacy, regulatory, or access requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Privacy-Enhancing Technology (PET)**           | Technology designed to reduce privacy risks while enabling legitimate data use.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Privacy-Enhancing Technologies**               | Technologies such as encryption, anonymization, pseudonymization, secure multiparty computation, differential privacy, and related techniques that reduce privacy risks.                                                                                                                                |
| **Differential Privacy**                         | Mathematical technique for limiting the information that can be inferred about individuals from statistical datasets or analyses.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Federated Learning**                           | Machine-learning approach where models are trained across distributed datasets without necessarily centralizing the underlying data.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Homomorphic Encryption**                       | Encryption technique allowing certain computations to be performed on encrypted data without first decrypting it.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC)**        | Cryptographic approach allowing multiple parties to jointly compute results without revealing their private inputs to one another.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Synthetic Data**                               | Artificially generated data designed to reproduce useful characteristics of real datasets without directly representing actual individuals.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Data Localization**                            | Requirement or practice of storing or processing data within a specified geographic jurisdiction.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Data Residency**                               | Geographic location where data is stored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Data Sovereignty**                             | Legal concept concerning which jurisdiction's laws apply to data based on its location or other factors.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Data Localization Requirement**                | Legal, regulatory, contractual, or organizational requirement restricting where data may be stored or processed.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Privacy Notice Layering**                      | Practice of providing privacy information in multiple layers so individuals can access concise information first and more detailed information as needed.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Transparency**                                 | Providing individuals with clear, accessible, and understandable information about personal-data processing.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Fair Processing**                              | Processing personal data in a manner that is reasonable, non-deceptive, and consistent with individuals' reasonable expectations and applicable law.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Data Protection Transparency**                 | Organizational practice of clearly communicating processing purposes, legal bases, recipients, retention, rights, and other required information.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Preference Center**                    | Interface allowing individuals to manage privacy preferences and, where applicable, consent choices.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Cookie Consent**                               | Consent mechanism used to obtain and record user choices regarding certain types of cookies or similar technologies where required.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Cookie Management**                            | Processes and technologies for controlling, documenting, and managing website cookies and tracking technologies.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Tracking Technology**                          | Technology used to monitor user behavior, activity, devices, or interactions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Direct Marketing**                             | Communication or activity intended to promote products, services, organizations, or interests directly to individuals.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Marketing Consent**                            | Consent for marketing activities where consent is the applicable legal requirement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Privacy-Preserving Analytics**                 | Analytical techniques designed to derive useful insights while reducing unnecessary exposure of personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Data Governance**                              | Framework for managing data ownership, quality, security, privacy, lifecycle, access, and accountability.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Data Quality**                                 | Degree to which data is accurate, complete, consistent, timely, relevant, and fit for purpose.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Data Accuracy**                                | Degree to which data correctly represents the relevant information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Data Integrity**                               | Assurance that data remains accurate, complete, consistent, and protected from unauthorized alteration.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Data Classification**                          | Categorization of information according to sensitivity, confidentiality, criticality, regulatory requirements, or business value.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Privacy Classification**                       | Classification of information according to privacy sensitivity and applicable protection requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Confidential Data**                            | Information requiring protection against unauthorized access or disclosure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Personal Data Mapping**                        | Process of identifying where personal data is collected, stored, processed, transmitted, and shared.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Privacy Data Flow**                            | Movement of personal data through systems, applications, business processes, users, and third parties.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Data Processing Inventory**                    | Structured inventory of processing activities, purposes, data categories, recipients, systems, locations, and safeguards.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Third-Party Privacy Risk**                     | Risk that an external party fails to appropriately protect or process personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Vendor Privacy Assessment**                    | Assessment of a third party's privacy practices, controls, compliance, and processing risks.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Privacy Due Diligence**                        | Review of a third party, product, service, acquisition, or processing activity to identify privacy risks and obligations.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Privacy Contractual Controls**                 | Privacy requirements established through contracts, DPAs, security agreements, and other legal instruments.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Data Protection Clause**                       | Contractual provision establishing requirements for personal-data processing and protection.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Subprocessor Due Diligence**                   | Assessment of subprocessors used by a processor to determine whether they meet required privacy and security obligations.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Privacy Assurance**                            | Activities providing confidence that privacy controls, processes, and obligations are operating effectively.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Privacy Audit**                                | Systematic examination of privacy practices, controls, processes, records, and compliance.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Privacy Compliance Assessment**                | Evaluation of an organization's privacy practices against applicable requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Privacy Control Testing**                      | Evaluation of whether privacy controls are properly designed and operating effectively.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Privacy Evidence**                             | Documentation demonstrating implementation or operation of privacy controls and compliance activities.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Privacy KPI**                                  | Key performance indicator measuring the performance or effectiveness of a privacy program.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

# Core GDPR Privacy Principles

For your GRC knowledge base, the GDPR principles should be remembered as:

### 1. Lawfulness, Fairness and Transparency

Personal data must be processed:

**Lawfully**
+
**Fairly**
+
**Transparently**

---

### 2. Purpose Limitation

Collect data for:

> **Specified + Explicit + Legitimate purposes**

and avoid incompatible secondary use.

---

### 3. Data Minimization

Use:

> **Adequate + Relevant + Necessary**

personal data.

Do not collect data simply because it might be useful someday.

---

### 4. Accuracy

Personal data should be:

> **Accurate + Current where necessary**

---

### 5. Storage Limitation

Do not retain personal data longer than necessary.

---

### 6. Integrity and Confidentiality

Protect data against:

* unauthorized access;
* unauthorized disclosure;
* accidental loss;
* destruction;
* alteration; and
* unlawful processing.

---

### 7. Accountability

The organization must not only comply but also be able to:

> **Demonstrate compliance.**

This is particularly important from a **GRC perspective**.

# GDPR Legal Bases

A useful memorization model is:

| Legal Basis              | Core Question                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Consent**              | Did the individual validly agree?                                           |
| **Contract**             | Is processing necessary to perform a contract?                              |
| **Legal Obligation**     | Is processing required by law?                                              |
| **Vital Interests**      | Is processing necessary to protect vital interests?                         |
| **Public Task**          | Is processing necessary for a public-interest task or official authority?   |
| **Legitimate Interests** | Is there a legitimate interest that is not overridden by individual rights? |

For legitimate interests:

**Purpose Test**

→ Is there a legitimate interest?

**Necessity Test**

→ Is processing necessary?

**Balancing Test**

→ Do the individual's interests, rights, or freedoms override the legitimate interest?

This is commonly documented through a **Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)**.

# Controller vs. Processor

This is one of the most important privacy concepts for GRC.

### Controller

Determines:

> **Why** personal data is processed

and generally determines:

> **How** it is processed within the relevant legal framework.

### Processor

Processes personal data:

> **On behalf of the controller.**

Simplified:

**Business decides purpose**

→ **Controller**

**External service performs processing**

→ **Processor**

Example:

**Company**

→ determines why customer data is processed

→ **Controller**

**Cloud/SaaS provider**

→ processes data on behalf of company

→ **Processor**

The actual legal classification depends on the facts and contractual arrangement.

# Personal Data vs. Sensitive Data

A useful conceptual hierarchy is:

**Data**

↓

**Personal Data**

↓

**Special Categories / Highly Sensitive Data**

Examples of personal data:

* name;

* phone number;
* IP address in relevant circumstances;
* identification number;

* online identifiers.

Examples of GDPR special categories:

* health data;
* genetic data;
* biometric data used for identification;
* racial or ethnic origin;
* political opinions;
* religious or philosophical beliefs;
* trade-union membership;
* sex life or sexual orientation.

# Pseudonymization vs. Anonymization

| Pseudonymization                                                                  | Anonymization                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data can potentially be linked back to an individual using additional information | Data is intended to no longer identify an individual            |
| Additional information is kept separately                                         | No reasonably available identification mechanism should remain  |
| Data remains personal data under GDPR                                             | Properly anonymized data falls outside GDPR personal-data scope |
| Reduces risk                                                                      | Removes identifiability when genuinely achieved                 |

**Key GRC point:**

> **Pseudonymized data is still personal data under GDPR.**

# Privacy and Cybersecurity Relationship

Privacy and cybersecurity overlap, but they are **not identical**.

### Cybersecurity primarily asks:

> **How do we protect information and systems?**

### Privacy primarily asks:

> **Should we collect, use, share, retain, or otherwise process this personal data, and are we doing so lawfully and fairly?**

Therefore:

**Cybersecurity**

→ Confidentiality
→ Integrity
→ Availability
→ Authentication
→ Access Control
→ Threat Protection

**Privacy**

→ Lawfulness
→ Purpose
→ Necessity
→ Transparency
→ Individual Rights
→ Data Minimization
→ Retention
→ Fairness

The two disciplines intersect through controls such as:

**Encryption + Access Control + DLP + Logging + Incident Response + Data Minimization + Pseudonymization**

The central privacy governance relationship is:

> **Data Collection → Purpose & Legal Basis → Data Minimization → Processing → Protection → Retention → Data Subject Rights → Secure Disposal → Accountability**
