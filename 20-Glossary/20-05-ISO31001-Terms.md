# 20.5 ISO 31000 Terms

This section defines the core terminology associated with **ISO 31000 Risk Management**, including risk principles, framework, process, risk assessment, treatment, monitoring, communication, and organizational integration.

> **Important terminology note:** ISO 31000:2018 uses a deliberately broad definition of risk: **“effect of uncertainty on objectives.”** Its terminology should therefore be understood as an enterprise-wide risk management vocabulary rather than only a cybersecurity risk vocabulary.

## ISO 31000 Glossary

| Term                                                                                    | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ISO 31000**                                                                           | An international standard providing principles and guidelines for managing risk across organizations and activities.                                                                                        |
| **Risk**                                                                                | The effect of uncertainty on objectives.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Risk Management**                                                                     | Coordinated activities to direct and control an organization with regard to risk.                                                                                                                           |
| **Risk Management Framework**                                                           | A set of components that provide the foundations and organizational arrangements for designing, implementing, monitoring, reviewing, and continually improving risk management throughout the organization. |
| **Risk Management Process**                                                             | The systematic application of policies, procedures, and practices to communication and consultation, establishing the context, assessing, treating, monitoring, reviewing, recording, and reporting risk.   |
| **Risk Management Principles**                                                          | Fundamental principles that guide effective risk management and help ensure that risk management creates and protects value.                                                                                |
| **Integrated**                                                                          | Risk management being embedded into all organizational activities and decision-making rather than operating as a separate function.                                                                         |
| **Structured**                                                                          | A systematic and consistent approach to risk management that produces comparable and reliable results.                                                                                                      |
| **Comprehensive**                                                                       | Risk management considering all relevant sources, categories, and dimensions of risk.                                                                                                                       |
| **Customized**                                                                          | Risk management being tailored to the organization's internal and external context, objectives, and needs.                                                                                                  |
| **Inclusive**                                                                           | Appropriate and timely involvement of stakeholders in risk management.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Dynamic**                                                                             | Risk management recognizing that risks can emerge, change, disappear, or evolve as internal and external circumstances change.                                                                              |
| **Best Available Information**                                                          | Risk decisions being based on relevant and reliable information while recognizing limitations, uncertainty, assumptions, and data quality.                                                                  |
| **Human and Cultural Factors**                                                          | Recognition that human behavior, organizational culture, perceptions, capabilities, and values influence risk management.                                                                                   |
| **Continual Improvement**                                                               | Ongoing enhancement of the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the risk management framework and process.                                                                                           |
| **Value Creation and Protection**                                                       | The principle that risk management should contribute to achieving objectives, improving performance, encouraging innovation, and protecting organizational value.                                           |
| **Leadership and Commitment**                                                           | Senior leadership's responsibility to establish direction, integrate risk management, allocate resources, assign responsibilities, and demonstrate commitment to effective risk management.                 |
| **Integration**                                                                         | Embedding risk management into governance, strategy, planning, management, reporting, processes, and organizational operations.                                                                             |
| **Framework Design**                                                                    | The process of determining how the risk management framework will be structured and adapted to the organization.                                                                                            |
| **Understanding the Organization and Its Context**                                      | Identifying internal and external factors relevant to the organization's objectives and risk management arrangements.                                                                                       |
| **Articulating Risk Management Commitment**                                             | Formal expression of leadership's commitment to establishing and maintaining effective risk management.                                                                                                     |
| **Assigning Organizational Roles, Authorities, Responsibilities, and Accountabilities** | Defining who has responsibility, authority, and accountability for risk management activities and decisions.                                                                                                |
| **Allocating Resources**                                                                | Providing sufficient people, skills, technology, information, funding, and other resources required for effective risk management.                                                                          |
| **Establishing Communication and Consultation**                                         | Creating appropriate mechanisms for sharing information and engaging stakeholders throughout risk management activities.                                                                                    |
| **Implementing Risk Management**                                                        | Putting the designed risk management framework and process into practical operation.                                                                                                                        |
| **Evaluating Framework Effectiveness**                                                  | Assessing whether the risk management framework is achieving its intended purpose and outcomes.                                                                                                             |
| **Continually Improving the Framework**                                                 | Making ongoing changes to improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the risk management framework.                                                                                            |
| **Communication**                                                                       | Providing information about risk to relevant stakeholders and decision-makers.                                                                                                                              |
| **Consultation**                                                                        | Seeking feedback and engaging stakeholders to support informed risk management decisions.                                                                                                                   |
| **Stakeholder**                                                                         | A person or organization that can affect, be affected by, or perceive itself to be affected by a decision or activity.                                                                                      |
| **Internal Context**                                                                    | The internal environment and factors that influence how the organization manages risk and achieves its objectives.                                                                                          |
| **External Context**                                                                    | The external environment and factors that influence how the organization manages risk and achieves its objectives.                                                                                          |
| **Risk Criteria**                                                                       | Terms of reference against which the significance of risk is evaluated.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Establishing the Context**                                                            | Defining the external and internal parameters to be considered when managing risk and establishing the scope and criteria for the risk management process.                                                  |
| **Risk Assessment**                                                                     | The overall process of risk identification, risk analysis, and risk evaluation.                                                                                                                             |
| **Risk Identification**                                                                 | The process of finding, recognizing, and describing risks.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Risk Analysis**                                                                       | The process of understanding the nature of risk and determining its level.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Risk Evaluation**                                                                     | The process of comparing the results of risk analysis with established risk criteria to determine whether additional action is required.                                                                    |
| **Risk Treatment**                                                                      | The process of modifying risk.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Risk Treatment Option**                                                               | An approach selected to modify risk, such as avoiding, removing, changing, sharing, or retaining risk.                                                                                                      |
| **Risk Avoidance**                                                                      | A decision not to engage in or continue an activity that creates unacceptable risk.                                                                                                                         |
| **Risk Removal**                                                                        | Eliminating the source of a risk.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Risk Modification**                                                                   | Changing the likelihood, consequences, or other characteristics of a risk.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Risk Sharing**                                                                        | Sharing the risk with another party or parties through arrangements such as contracts, insurance, partnerships, or other mechanisms.                                                                        |
| **Risk Retention**                                                                      | Accepting or retaining responsibility for a particular risk.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Residual Risk**                                                                       | The risk remaining after risk treatment.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Risk Owner**                                                                          | The person or entity with accountability and authority to manage a risk.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Risk Source**                                                                         | An element that alone or in combination has the intrinsic potential to give rise to risk.                                                                                                                   |
| **Event**                                                                               | An occurrence or change of a particular set of circumstances.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Risk Event**                                                                          | An occurrence that can affect the achievement of objectives and contribute to consequences.                                                                                                                 |
| **Consequence**                                                                         | The outcome of an event affecting objectives.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Likelihood**                                                                          | The chance of something happening.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Probability**                                                                         | A measure or expression of the likelihood of occurrence.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Risk Level**                                                                          | The magnitude of a risk expressed in terms of the combination of consequences and their likelihood.                                                                                                         |
| **Risk Profile**                                                                        | A description of a set of risks.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Risk Appetite**                                                                       | The organization's willingness to pursue, retain, or take risk in order to achieve its objectives.                                                                                                          |
| **Risk Tolerance**                                                                      | An organization's or stakeholder's readiness to bear risk after risk treatment in order to achieve objectives.                                                                                              |
| **Risk Capacity**                                                                       | The maximum amount or level of risk an organization can absorb before its ability to achieve objectives or remain viable is seriously threatened.                                                           |
| **Risk Treatment Plan**                                                                 | A documented plan specifying how identified risks will be treated, including actions, responsibilities, resources, and timelines.                                                                           |
| **Monitoring**                                                                          | Continual or periodic checking, supervising, critically observing, or determining the status of a risk or risk management activity.                                                                         |
| **Review**                                                                              | An activity undertaken to determine the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the subject matter being examined to achieve established objectives.                                                    |
| **Risk Monitoring**                                                                     | Tracking identified risks, risk levels, controls, treatments, changes, and relevant indicators over time.                                                                                                   |
| **Risk Review**                                                                         | Periodic or event-driven examination of risks, assumptions, controls, treatments, and risk criteria.                                                                                                        |
| **Risk Reporting**                                                                      | Communicating information about risk, risk management activities, and their outcomes to relevant stakeholders.                                                                                              |
| **Recording**                                                                           | Creating and maintaining documented information about risk management activities and results.                                                                                                               |
| **Risk Register**                                                                       | A structured record of identified risks and associated information such as causes, consequences, owners, treatments, ratings, and status.                                                                   |
| **Risk Treatment Plan**                                                                 | A documented set of actions intended to modify identified risks and achieve the desired risk state.                                                                                                         |
| **Risk Criteria**                                                                       | The criteria used to evaluate the significance of risk and determine whether risk treatment is required.                                                                                                    |
| **Risk Matrix**                                                                         | A tool used to display or compare risk levels according to defined dimensions such as likelihood and consequence.                                                                                           |
| **Risk Rating**                                                                         | A representation of the assessed level or significance of risk based on established criteria.                                                                                                               |
| **Risk Aggregation**                                                                    | Combining individual risks to understand their collective exposure and potential effect on objectives.                                                                                                      |
| **Risk Interdependency**                                                                | A relationship in which risks can influence, trigger, amplify, or modify one another.                                                                                                                       |
| **Emerging Risk**                                                                       | A new, changing, or poorly understood risk whose potential consequences may become significant as circumstances develop.                                                                                    |
| **Risk Communication**                                                                  | The exchange of information concerning risk among decision-makers, stakeholders, and other relevant parties.                                                                                                |
| **Risk Culture**                                                                        | The shared values, beliefs, attitudes, knowledge, and behaviors influencing how an organization understands and manages risk.                                                                               |
| **Risk Awareness**                                                                      | Understanding of risks, their potential consequences, and the responsibilities associated with managing them.                                                                                               |
| **Risk-Based Decision Making**                                                          | Decision-making that explicitly considers relevant risks, uncertainty, consequences, and organizational objectives.                                                                                         |
| **Risk-Based Approach**                                                                 | An approach that prioritizes activities and resources according to the significance and characteristics of risk.                                                                                            |
| **Risk Treatment Effectiveness**                                                        | The extent to which risk treatment successfully modifies risk as intended.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Risk Management Effectiveness**                                                       | The degree to which risk management arrangements achieve their intended objectives and support organizational outcomes.                                                                                     |

# ISO 31000 Risk Management Principles

ISO 31000:2018 establishes **eight principles** that provide the foundation for effective risk management.

| Principle                        | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Integrated**                   | Risk management should be an integral part of organizational activities and decision-making.                   |
| **Structured and Comprehensive** | A systematic and comprehensive approach should produce consistent and comparable results.                      |
| **Customized**                   | The risk management approach should be appropriate to the organization's context and objectives.               |
| **Inclusive**                    | Relevant stakeholders should participate appropriately and in a timely manner.                                 |
| **Dynamic**                      | Risk management should recognize and respond to changes, emerging risks, and changing circumstances.           |
| **Best Available Information**   | Decisions should use relevant, reliable, and timely information while recognizing uncertainty and limitations. |
| **Human and Cultural Factors**   | Human behavior, culture, perceptions, and capabilities should be considered.                                   |
| **Continual Improvement**        | Risk management should continuously improve through learning, experience, and adaptation.                      |

A critical concept is that ISO 31000 risk management should **create and protect value**, rather than simply prevent negative events.

# Key ISO 31000 Distinctions

## 1. Risk vs. Threat

ISO 31000 defines **risk** broadly as:

> **Effect of uncertainty on objectives**

A **threat** is a potential source or cause of harm.

For example:

**Threat:** ransomware attack
**Vulnerability:** unpatched endpoint
**Event:** ransomware infection
**Consequence:** operational disruption
**Risk:** uncertainty regarding the effect of the event on business objectives.

This distinction is particularly important when integrating **ISO 31000 with ISO/IEC 27001**.

---

## 2. Risk Identification vs. Risk Analysis vs. Risk Evaluation

These are three separate stages of risk assessment:

### Risk Identification

Determines:

> **What can happen?**

### Risk Analysis

Determines:

> **How likely is it and what could happen?**

### Risk Evaluation

Determines:

> **Is the resulting risk acceptable, tolerable, or in need of treatment?**

Therefore:

**Identification → Analysis → Evaluation = Risk Assessment**

---

## 3. Risk Assessment vs. Risk Treatment

**Risk Assessment** determines the nature and significance of risks.

**Risk Treatment** determines what should be done about them.

For example:

> Risk assessment identifies that a critical supplier presents high operational risk.

Risk treatment could involve:

* additional security requirements;
* contractual controls;
* business continuity requirements;
* supplier monitoring;
* alternative suppliers;
* insurance;
* reducing dependency; or
* accepting the risk within approved criteria.

---

## 4. Consequence vs. Likelihood

**Consequence** describes **what happens if the event occurs**.

**Likelihood** describes **the chance of the event occurring**.

A common risk-rating approach is conceptually:

**Risk Level ≈ Likelihood × Consequence**

However, ISO 31000 does **not require a specific numerical risk matrix or scoring formula**. Organizations should establish risk criteria appropriate to their context.

---

## 5. Risk Source vs. Event vs. Consequence

These should be distinguished:

**Risk Source**
→ Something that has the potential to create risk.

**Event**
→ Something that occurs or changes circumstances.

**Consequence**
→ The resulting effect on objectives.

Example:

> **Risk source:** dependence on a single cloud provider
> **Event:** extended cloud service outage
> **Consequence:** customer service disruption and financial loss

---

## 6. Risk Appetite vs. Risk Tolerance

**Risk appetite** represents the organization's willingness to pursue or retain risk.

**Risk tolerance** represents the acceptable level of variation or exposure that the organization is prepared to bear.

A practical distinction is:

> **Risk appetite = How much risk are we willing to take?**
> **Risk tolerance = How much deviation are we willing to accept?**

---

## 7. Risk Treatment Options

ISO 31000 recognizes multiple approaches to modifying risk.

These include:

**Avoid the risk**
→ Stop or do not undertake the activity.

**Remove the risk source**
→ Eliminate the underlying source.

**Modify the risk**
→ Change likelihood and/or consequences.

**Share the risk**
→ Allocate or distribute risk with another party.

**Retain the risk**
→ Accept or retain the risk based on informed decision-making.

Risk treatment may use multiple options simultaneously.

# ISO 31000 and ISO/IEC 27001 Relationship

ISO 31000 provides a **general risk management framework and vocabulary** that can be applied across an organization.

ISO/IEC 27001 applies risk management specifically to **information security and the ISMS**.

A useful GRC relationship is:

**ISO 31000**
→ Enterprise-wide risk management principles and framework

**ISO/IEC 27001**
→ Information security management system and information security risk management

**ISO/IEC 27005**
→ Guidance specifically supporting information security risk management

This means an organization can use ISO 31000 as its **enterprise risk management foundation** while applying ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27005 within its cybersecurity and information security domains.
