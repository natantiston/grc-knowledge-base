# 20.11 Third-Party Risk Terms

This section defines the core terminology associated with **Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), Vendor Risk Management (VRM), Supplier Risk Management, Outsourcing Risk, ICT Third-Party Risk, Cloud Risk, Supply Chain Risk, and Third-Party GRC**.

> **Terminology note:** Third-party risk terminology varies across frameworks. This glossary is designed for the GRC knowledge base and is broadly aligned with concepts found in **ISO 27001, ISO 31000, NIST, COBIT, PCI DSS, DORA, NIS2, SOC 2, and common enterprise TPRM practices**.

---

# Third-Party Risk Glossary

| Term                                                | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Third Party**                                     | An external individual or organization that provides products, services, technology, data, infrastructure, or other capabilities to an organization.                                                                 |
| **Third-Party Risk**                                | The possibility that an external party's actions, failures, weaknesses, or dependencies could negatively affect an organization's security, operations, compliance, finances, reputation, resilience, or objectives. |
| **Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)**              | The structured process of identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and governing risks associated with third parties throughout their relationship with an organization.                                        |
| **Vendor Risk Management (VRM)**                    | The process of managing risks associated with vendors and suppliers.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Supplier Risk Management**                        | The process of identifying and managing risks associated with suppliers and external providers.                                                                                                                      |
| **Third-Party Governance**                          | The policies, roles, processes, oversight mechanisms, and decision rights used to manage third-party relationships and risks.                                                                                        |
| **Third-Party Risk Framework**                      | A structured set of policies, processes, controls, assessment methods, and governance mechanisms used to manage third-party risk.                                                                                    |
| **Third-Party Risk Policy**                         | A formally approved policy defining organizational requirements for identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring, and terminating third-party relationships.                                                         |
| **Third-Party Risk Appetite**                       | The amount and type of third-party risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.                                                                                                           |
| **Third-Party Risk Tolerance**                      | The acceptable level of variation or exposure associated with a particular third-party risk.                                                                                                                         |
| **Third-Party Risk Assessment**                     | A structured evaluation of risks associated with a third party, service, product, relationship, or dependency.                                                                                                       |
| **Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA)**                    | An assessment performed to evaluate the security, privacy, compliance, operational, financial, and other risks associated with a vendor.                                                                             |
| **Supplier Assessment**                             | An evaluation of a supplier's capabilities, controls, risks, compliance, resilience, and ability to meet contractual requirements.                                                                                   |
| **Inherent Risk**                                   | The level of risk associated with a third-party relationship before considering mitigating controls.                                                                                                                 |
| **Residual Risk**                                   | The risk remaining after applicable controls and mitigation measures have been implemented.                                                                                                                          |
| **Control Risk**                                    | Risk arising from the absence, weakness, failure, or ineffective operation of controls.                                                                                                                              |
| **Concentration Risk**                              | Risk resulting from excessive dependence on one third party, provider, technology, geographic region, or supply chain.                                                                                               |
| **Fourth-Party Risk**                               | Risk arising from a third party's own suppliers, subcontractors, service providers, or other external dependencies.                                                                                                  |
| **Nth-Party Risk**                                  | Risk arising from entities several levels removed from the organization's direct contractual relationship.                                                                                                           |
| **Subcontractor**                                   | An external party engaged by a third party to perform part of the contracted service.                                                                                                                                |
| **Subprocessor**                                    | A third party engaged by a data processor to process personal data on behalf of the controller or processor.                                                                                                         |
| **Downstream Provider**                             | An external provider supporting another supplier's delivery of services to the organization.                                                                                                                         |
| **Critical Third Party**                            | A third party whose failure, compromise, or disruption could cause significant impact to the organization's operations, customers, security, compliance, or resilience.                                              |
| **Critical Supplier**                               | A supplier providing products or services considered essential to important organizational processes or functions.                                                                                                   |
| **Critical Service Provider**                       | A service provider whose disruption could materially affect important business or technology services.                                                                                                               |
| **Material Third Party**                            | A third party whose relationship has significant financial, operational, regulatory, security, or strategic importance.                                                                                              |
| **High-Risk Third Party**                           | A third party whose inherent or residual risk exceeds defined organizational thresholds.                                                                                                                             |
| **Low-Risk Third Party**                            | A third party whose assessed risk is within a low-risk category according to defined criteria.                                                                                                                       |
| **Medium-Risk Third Party**                         | A third party whose assessed risk falls within an intermediate risk category.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Third-Party Tiering**                             | Categorizing third parties according to risk, criticality, service type, data access, regulatory impact, or other defined criteria.                                                                                  |
| **Risk-Based Tiering**                              | Assigning third-party risk levels based on the assessed risk and business impact of the relationship.                                                                                                                |
| **Vendor Criticality**                              | The degree to which an organization's operations, services, customers, or objectives depend on a vendor.                                                                                                             |
| **Business Criticality**                            | The importance of the outsourced product or service to business operations and objectives.                                                                                                                           |
| **Data Criticality**                                | The sensitivity and importance of information accessed, processed, stored, or transmitted by a third party.                                                                                                          |
| **Service Criticality**                             | The importance of a third-party service to organizational operations, customers, regulatory obligations, or resilience.                                                                                              |
| **Third-Party Inventory**                           | A maintained record of third parties, suppliers, vendors, services, owners, risk classifications, contracts, and assessment status.                                                                                  |
| **Vendor Inventory**                                | A centralized register of vendors and associated information.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Supplier Register**                               | A maintained record of suppliers, services, owners, contracts, risk ratings, and relevant controls.                                                                                                                  |
| **Third-Party Relationship**                        | The formal or informal business relationship between an organization and an external party.                                                                                                                          |
| **Third-Party Lifecycle**                           | The sequence of stages from identification and onboarding through contracting, monitoring, renewal, and termination.                                                                                                 |
| **Third-Party Onboarding**                          | The process of evaluating and approving a third party before the organization begins or expands the relationship.                                                                                                    |
| **Vendor Due Diligence**                            | Investigation and evaluation of a third party's capabilities, ownership, financial condition, security, compliance, reputation, and other relevant characteristics before engagement.                                |
| **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**                    | Additional investigation performed for high-risk, critical, sensitive, or otherwise significant third parties.                                                                                                       |
| **Vendor Screening**                                | Initial review of a potential vendor against defined business, security, compliance, legal, financial, and risk criteria.                                                                                            |
| **Vendor Selection**                                | The process of evaluating and selecting a third party based on defined business, technical, security, financial, and risk requirements.                                                                              |
| **Vendor Approval**                                 | Formal authorization to establish or continue a third-party relationship.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Risk Acceptance**                                 | Formal acknowledgment and approval of a residual third-party risk by an authorized risk owner.                                                                                                                       |
| **Risk Exception**                                  | Formal authorization to temporarily or conditionally deviate from an established third-party security or risk requirement.                                                                                           |
| **Risk Mitigation**                                 | Actions designed to reduce the likelihood or impact of third-party risk.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Risk Transfer**                                   | Shifting some financial or contractual consequences of risk to another party, such as through insurance or contractual arrangements.                                                                                 |
| **Risk Avoidance**                                  | Eliminating the risk by deciding not to establish or continue the third-party relationship or activity.                                                                                                              |
| **Risk Sharing**                                    | Sharing risk consequences or responsibilities between multiple parties.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Third-Party Offboarding**                         | The controlled process for terminating a third-party relationship and removing associated access, data, systems, credentials, and dependencies.                                                                      |
| **Vendor Termination**                              | Formal ending of a contractual relationship with a vendor.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Exit Strategy**                                   | A documented plan for safely transitioning away from a third-party provider.                                                                                                                                         |
| **Exit Plan**                                       | Specific procedures for terminating or transitioning outsourced services while maintaining business continuity, security, and compliance.                                                                            |
| **Exit Assistance**                                 | Contractually defined support that a provider must give during service transition or termination.                                                                                                                    |
| **Transition Plan**                                 | A documented plan for moving services, data, systems, or responsibilities from one provider to another or back in-house.                                                                                             |
| **Vendor Lock-In**                                  | A condition in which switching providers is difficult, costly, risky, or impractical because of technical, contractual, financial, or operational dependencies.                                                      |
| **Portability**                                     | The ability to move data, applications, configurations, or services between providers or environments.                                                                                                               |
| **Interoperability**                                | The ability of systems, applications, or services to operate and exchange information effectively with other systems.                                                                                                |
| **Contract**                                        | A legally binding agreement defining rights, responsibilities, obligations, services, and requirements between parties.                                                                                              |
| **Master Services Agreement (MSA)**                 | A primary contractual agreement establishing general terms and conditions for an ongoing relationship.                                                                                                               |
| **Statement of Work (SOW)**                         | A document defining specific services, deliverables, responsibilities, timelines, and other requirements for a particular engagement.                                                                                |
| **Service Level Agreement (SLA)**                   | A contractual agreement defining expected service performance, availability, response times, and related service commitments.                                                                                        |
| **Operating Level Agreement (OLA)**                 | An internal or inter-organizational agreement defining operational responsibilities and service expectations supporting an SLA.                                                                                      |
| **Service Level Objective (SLO)**                   | A specific measurable target for a service characteristic such as availability, response time, or recovery time.                                                                                                     |
| **Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**                 | A measurable indicator used to evaluate business or service performance.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Key Risk Indicator (KRI)**                        | A measurable indicator used to identify changes or trends in risk exposure.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Contractual Security Requirement**                | A security obligation formally incorporated into a third-party contract.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Security Addendum**                               | A contractual document containing additional information-security requirements applicable to a third party.                                                                                                          |
| **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**                 | A contract defining requirements for processing personal data by a processor or other third party.                                                                                                                   |
| **Confidentiality Agreement**                       | An agreement requiring parties to protect confidential information from unauthorized disclosure.                                                                                                                     |
| **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**                  | A legally binding agreement restricting unauthorized disclosure or use of confidential information.                                                                                                                  |
| **Right to Audit**                                  | A contractual provision allowing an organization or authorized party to evaluate a third party's compliance with defined requirements.                                                                               |
| **Audit Rights**                                    | Contractual rights permitting assessment, inspection, testing, or review of a third party's controls and compliance.                                                                                                 |
| **Right to Assess**                                 | A contractual right to perform or request assessments of a third party's security, compliance, or risk controls.                                                                                                     |
| **Contractual Obligation**                          | A requirement that a third party is legally obligated to fulfill under a contract.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Security Clause**                                 | A contractual provision establishing information-security requirements.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Breach Notification Clause**                      | A contractual requirement defining how and when a third party must notify the organization of a security or privacy incident.                                                                                        |
| **Incident Notification**                           | Communication from a third party concerning a security, privacy, operational, or compliance incident.                                                                                                                |
| **Data Breach**                                     | Unauthorized access to, disclosure of, alteration of, or loss of protected information.                                                                                                                              |
| **Security Incident**                               | An event that actually or potentially compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information or systems.                                                                                         |
| **Third-Party Incident**                            | A security, privacy, operational, or compliance incident involving a third party or its environment.                                                                                                                 |
| **Vendor Incident Response**                        | Processes for coordinating response to incidents involving third-party providers.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Third-Party Business Continuity**                 | The capability of a third party to continue delivering essential services during disruption.                                                                                                                         |
| **Vendor Business Continuity Plan (BCP)**           | A third party's documented plan for maintaining or restoring critical services during disruptive events.                                                                                                             |
| **Vendor Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)**             | A third party's documented procedures for recovering technology and services following a disruptive event.                                                                                                           |
| **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**                   | The targeted maximum time within which a service or capability should be restored after disruption.                                                                                                                  |
| **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**                  | The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)**                | The maximum period an organization can tolerate a service or function being unavailable before unacceptable consequences occur.                                                                                      |
| **Resilience**                                      | The ability to withstand, adapt to, respond to, and recover from disruptions.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Third-Party Resilience**                          | The ability of a third party to maintain or rapidly restore contracted services during disruptions.                                                                                                                  |
| **Operational Resilience**                          | The ability of an organization and its critical dependencies to continue delivering important business services during disruption.                                                                                   |
| **ICT Third-Party Risk**                            | Risk arising from external information and communications technology providers and dependencies.                                                                                                                     |
| **ICT Service Provider**                            | A third party providing information and communications technology services.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Cloud Service Provider (CSP)**                    | A third party providing cloud-based infrastructure, platforms, applications, storage, computing, or related services.                                                                                                |
| **Software as a Service (SaaS)**                    | A model in which software is delivered and operated by a provider and accessed by customers as a service.                                                                                                            |
| **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**                    | A cloud service model providing managed application-development and deployment capabilities.                                                                                                                         |
| **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**              | A cloud service model providing virtualized computing, storage, networking, and related infrastructure capabilities.                                                                                                 |
| **Managed Service Provider (MSP)**                  | A third party that manages technology or operational services on behalf of an organization.                                                                                                                          |
| **Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP)**        | A third party providing managed cybersecurity services such as monitoring, detection, response, or security operations.                                                                                              |
| **Outsourcing**                                     | Contracting an external party to perform activities, processes, functions, or services previously performed or potentially performable internally.                                                                   |
| **Material Outsourcing**                            | Outsourcing of activities considered significant to business operations, regulatory compliance, resilience, or strategic objectives.                                                                                 |
| **Critical Outsourcing**                            | Outsourcing involving activities whose failure could cause significant operational, financial, regulatory, or customer impact.                                                                                       |
| **Sub-Outsourcing**                                 | An arrangement where an outsourced provider further delegates part of the outsourced service to another provider.                                                                                                    |
| **Supply Chain Risk**                               | Risk arising from dependencies on suppliers, vendors, service providers, technology providers, logistics providers, and other elements of the supply chain.                                                          |
| **Third-Party Cyber Risk**                          | Cybersecurity risk arising from third-party systems, services, personnel, technologies, access, or dependencies.                                                                                                     |
| **Supply Chain Cybersecurity**                      | Practices for protecting technology and information throughout the digital and technology supply chain.                                                                                                              |
| **Software Supply Chain**                           | The ecosystem of software components, libraries, dependencies, developers, repositories, build systems, and vendors involved in producing software.                                                                  |
| **Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)**               | A structured inventory of software components and dependencies contained within a software product.                                                                                                                  |
| **Open-Source Software (OSS)**                      | Software whose source code is made available under an applicable open-source license.                                                                                                                                |
| **Software Dependency**                             | A software component required by another software application or system to function.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Dependency Risk**                                 | Risk resulting from vulnerabilities, availability, licensing, integrity, or security issues in software or service dependencies.                                                                                     |
| **Supply Chain Attack**                             | An attack that compromises a trusted supplier, software component, service provider, or other dependency to affect downstream organizations.                                                                         |
| **Third-Party Access**                              | Access granted to external parties to organizational systems, networks, applications, data, or facilities.                                                                                                           |
| **Privileged Third-Party Access**                   | Elevated access granted to external parties that permits administrative or security-sensitive actions.                                                                                                               |
| **Remote Vendor Access**                            | Remote access provided to a third party for support, maintenance, administration, or other authorized activities.                                                                                                    |
| **Vendor Identity Management**                      | Processes for establishing, authenticating, authorizing, monitoring, and terminating identities associated with third-party personnel.                                                                               |
| **Third-Party Least Privilege**                     | Limiting third-party access to only the systems, data, and functions necessary to perform authorized responsibilities.                                                                                               |
| **Just-in-Time Vendor Access**                      | Temporary third-party access granted only when needed and for a defined period.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Privileged Access Management (PAM)**              | Controls for managing, monitoring, and securing privileged accounts and access.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Third-Party Authentication**                      | Verification of the identity of external users or systems before granting access.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Third-Party Segmentation**                        | Isolation of third-party access or systems to reduce the potential impact of compromise.                                                                                                                             |
| **Vendor Monitoring**                               | Ongoing monitoring of third-party risk, security posture, performance, compliance, incidents, and changes.                                                                                                           |
| **Continuous Monitoring**                           | Ongoing collection and analysis of information to identify changes in third-party risk or control effectiveness.                                                                                                     |
| **Continuous Threat Monitoring**                    | Ongoing monitoring for security threats, vulnerabilities, breaches, or adverse events involving third parties.                                                                                                       |
| **External Threat Intelligence**                    | Security information collected from external sources to identify threats affecting an organization or its third parties.                                                                                             |
| **Cyber Rating**                                    | An externally generated assessment or score intended to indicate aspects of a company's cybersecurity posture.                                                                                                       |
| **Security Rating**                                 | A rating representing an external or internal assessment of a third party's security posture.                                                                                                                        |
| **Vendor Security Rating**                          | Security assessment information used to monitor a vendor's cybersecurity posture.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Questionnaire-Based Assessment**                  | Third-party risk assessment performed through standardized questionnaires.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Evidence-Based Assessment**                       | Assessment relying on objective evidence such as policies, reports, certificates, logs, configurations, and audit results.                                                                                           |
| **Inherent Risk Questionnaire**                     | Questionnaire used to determine the inherent risk associated with a third-party relationship before considering controls.                                                                                            |
| **Security Questionnaire**                          | Questionnaire used to collect information about a third party's security policies, controls, practices, and certifications.                                                                                          |
| **CAIQ**                                            | Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire, a questionnaire developed by the Cloud Security Alliance for evaluating cloud security practices.                                                                    |
| **SIG**                                             | Standardized Information Gathering questionnaire framework commonly used for third-party risk assessments.                                                                                                           |
| **SOC 1**                                           | Service Organization Control report focused primarily on controls relevant to financial reporting.                                                                                                                   |
| **SOC 2**                                           | Independent examination of controls relating to Trust Services Criteria such as security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.                                                          |
| **SOC 2 Type I**                                    | Report evaluating the design of controls at a specific point in time.                                                                                                                                                |
| **SOC 2 Type II**                                   | Report evaluating both the design and operating effectiveness of controls over a defined period.                                                                                                                     |
| **ISO/IEC 27001 Certification**                     | Independent certification that an organization's information security management system meets applicable ISO/IEC 27001 requirements.                                                                                 |
| **Certificate of Insurance (COI)**                  | Evidence that a third party maintains specified insurance coverage.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Cyber Insurance**                                 | Insurance designed to provide financial protection against certain cyber-related losses.                                                                                                                             |
| **Professional Indemnity Insurance**                | Insurance covering certain professional errors, omissions, or failures.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Financial Viability**                             | The ability of a third party to remain financially stable and continue providing contracted services.                                                                                                                |
| **Financial Health Assessment**                     | Evaluation of a vendor's financial stability, liquidity, profitability, debt, and other relevant indicators.                                                                                                         |
| **Vendor Bankruptcy Risk**                          | Risk that a supplier's financial failure results in service disruption or loss of critical capabilities.                                                                                                             |
| **Geographic Risk**                                 | Risk associated with the geographic location of a third party, service, data center, personnel, or supply chain dependency.                                                                                          |
| **Country Risk**                                    | Risk arising from political, economic, legal, regulatory, social, or security conditions in a country.                                                                                                               |
| **Jurisdictional Risk**                             | Risk arising from the laws, regulations, government authorities, or legal requirements applicable to a third party or its operations.                                                                                |
| **Data Residency**                                  | The geographic location where data is stored or processed.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Data Sovereignty**                                | The concept that data is subject to the laws and jurisdiction of the country or territory where it is located or processed.                                                                                          |
| **Cross-Border Data Transfer**                      | Transfer of data between different countries or legal jurisdictions.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Regulatory Risk**                                 | Risk that a third-party relationship causes the organization to violate applicable laws, regulations, or supervisory requirements.                                                                                   |
| **Compliance Assessment**                           | Evaluation of a third party's compliance with specified legal, regulatory, contractual, or organizational requirements.                                                                                              |
| **Sanctions Screening**                             | Screening of third parties against applicable sanctions and restricted-party lists.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Adverse Media Screening**                         | Review of public information for negative events, allegations, controversies, litigation, fraud, cyber incidents, or other risk indicators associated with a third party.                                            |
| **Background Screening**                            | Evaluation of relevant information about third-party personnel or organizations before granting access or establishing relationships.                                                                                |
| **Conflict of Interest**                            | A situation where personal, financial, organizational, or other interests could improperly influence business decisions.                                                                                             |
| **Bribery and Corruption Risk**                     | Risk that a third party engages in bribery, corruption, improper payments, or unethical conduct.                                                                                                                     |
| **Third-Party Ethics Due Diligence**                | Assessment of a third party's ethical conduct, anti-bribery controls, conflicts of interest, and compliance practices.                                                                                               |
| **Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Risk** | Risk arising from environmental, social, governance, sustainability, human-rights, or related third-party practices.                                                                                                 |
| **Modern Slavery Risk**                             | Risk of forced labor, human trafficking, or other prohibited labor practices within a third party's operations or supply chain.                                                                                      |
| **Human Rights Due Diligence**                      | Assessment of whether third-party activities may create or contribute to human-rights violations.                                                                                                                    |
| **Fourth-Party Assessment**                         | Evaluation of significant subcontractors or other downstream dependencies used by a third party.                                                                                                                     |
| **Fourth-Party Visibility**                         | The degree to which an organization understands and can monitor its third parties' downstream providers.                                                                                                             |
| **Fourth-Party Concentration Risk**                 | Risk that multiple third parties rely on the same downstream provider, creating a hidden common dependency.                                                                                                          |
| **Dependency Mapping**                              | Identification and documentation of relationships between organizational services and third-party or fourth-party dependencies.                                                                                      |
| **Critical Dependency**                             | A third-party or technology dependency whose failure could significantly affect an important organizational service or process.                                                                                      |
| **Single Point of Failure (SPOF)**                  | A dependency whose failure could cause significant disruption because no adequate alternative exists.                                                                                                                |
| **Multi-Sourcing**                                  | Use of multiple providers for similar or related services to reduce dependency on a single provider.                                                                                                                 |
| **Dual Sourcing**                                   | Use of two providers for a service or capability to reduce concentration and availability risk.                                                                                                                      |
| **Alternative Supplier**                            | A provider capable of replacing an existing supplier if required.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Supplier Substitutability**                       | The feasibility and speed with which one supplier can be replaced by another.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Business Continuity Testing**                     | Testing performed to determine whether a third party can continue or restore services during disruption.                                                                                                             |
| **Disaster Recovery Testing**                       | Testing of technical recovery capabilities following a disruptive event.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Tabletop Exercise**                               | A discussion-based exercise used to evaluate response plans, responsibilities, decisions, and coordination.                                                                                                          |
| **Vendor Resilience Testing**                       | Testing designed to evaluate a third party's ability to maintain or restore critical services.                                                                                                                       |
| **Service Continuity**                              | Ability to continue delivering a service at an acceptable level during and after disruption.                                                                                                                         |
| **Contract Renewal Review**                         | Periodic reassessment performed before extending a third-party contract.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Periodic Risk Review**                            | Scheduled reassessment of third-party risk throughout the relationship lifecycle.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Trigger-Based Assessment**                        | Reassessment initiated by a specific event, such as a major incident, acquisition, regulatory change, material service change, or significant security finding.                                                      |
| **Material Change**                                 | A significant change to a third party, service, technology, ownership, location, subcontractor, data processing, or risk profile.                                                                                    |
| **Change Notification**                             | A requirement for a third party to notify the organization about relevant changes.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Control Attestation**                             | A formal statement by a third party asserting that specified controls or requirements are implemented.                                                                                                               |
| **Independent Assurance**                           | Assurance provided by an independent auditor, assessor, certification body, or other qualified party.                                                                                                                |
| **Third-Party Audit**                               | Formal examination of a third party's controls, processes, compliance, or performance.                                                                                                                               |
| **Vendor Audit**                                    | Audit of a vendor against contractual, security, regulatory, or organizational requirements.                                                                                                                         |
| **Remote Audit**                                    | Assessment performed remotely through documentation review, interviews, demonstrations, and technical evidence.                                                                                                      |
| **On-Site Assessment**                              | Assessment performed physically at a third party's facility or location.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Audit Finding**                                   | A condition identified during an assessment that represents a control weakness, nonconformity, or other issue.                                                                                                       |
| **Remediation Plan**                                | A documented plan for addressing identified third-party risks or control deficiencies.                                                                                                                               |
| **Corrective Action Plan (CAP)**                    | Formal plan defining actions, owners, deadlines, and evidence required to resolve identified deficiencies.                                                                                                           |
| **Issue Aging**                                     | Measurement of how long a third-party risk or control issue has remained unresolved.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Risk Acceptance Authority**                       | Person or governance body authorized to formally accept specified levels of third-party risk.                                                                                                                        |
| **Exception Management**                            | Process for requesting, evaluating, approving, documenting, monitoring, and closing deviations from third-party requirements.                                                                                        |
| **Third-Party Risk Dashboard**                      | Management reporting interface showing third-party risk, assessments, findings, incidents, performance, and compliance status.                                                                                       |
| **TPRM Key Risk Indicator (KRI)**                   | Metric used to identify changes in third-party risk exposure.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **TPRM Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**            | Metric used to measure effectiveness and efficiency of the third-party risk management program.                                                                                                                      |
| **Assessment Coverage**                             | Percentage or proportion of relevant third parties that have undergone required risk assessments.                                                                                                                    |
| **Critical Vendor Coverage**                        | Percentage of critical third parties subject to required risk assessments and monitoring.                                                                                                                            |
| **Overdue Assessment**                              | Third-party assessment that has exceeded its required completion or renewal date.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Open Finding**                                    | A third-party control deficiency or issue that has not yet been remediated or formally accepted.                                                                                                                     |
| **Risk Trend**                                      | Direction in which third-party risk exposure is changing over time.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Third-Party Risk Scoring**                        | Process of assigning quantitative or qualitative scores to third-party risk factors.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Risk Rating**                                     | Overall classification of a third party's assessed risk, such as low, medium, high, or critical.                                                                                                                     |
| **Risk Heat Map**                                   | Visual representation of third-party risks based on dimensions such as likelihood and impact.                                                                                                                        |
| **Residual Risk Acceptance**                        | Formal approval of risk remaining after controls and mitigation have been applied.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Continuous Assurance**                            | Ongoing collection and evaluation of evidence to provide confidence that third-party controls remain effective.                                                                                                      |
| **Continuous Compliance Monitoring**                | Ongoing monitoring of third-party compliance against applicable requirements.                                                                                                                                        |

# Third-Party Risk Lifecycle

A mature TPRM program generally follows this lifecycle:

**1. Identify**

→ Identify the third party
→ Identify services
→ Identify data
→ Identify systems
→ Identify dependencies

↓

**2. Classify**

→ Business criticality
→ Data sensitivity
→ Regulatory impact
→ Service criticality
→ Geographic exposure
→ Access level

↓

**3. Assess**

→ Inherent risk
→ Security risk
→ Privacy risk
→ Compliance risk
→ Operational risk
→ Financial risk
→ Resilience risk
→ Concentration risk

↓

**4. Due Diligence**

→ Questionnaires
→ Certifications
→ SOC reports
→ Penetration tests
→ Financial assessment
→ Legal review
→ Privacy assessment
→ Adverse media
→ Fourth-party assessment

↓

**5. Contract**

→ Security requirements
→ SLA
→ Data protection
→ Incident notification
→ Audit rights
→ Regulatory requirements
→ Business continuity
→ Exit requirements

↓

**6. Approve**

→ Risk rating
→ Risk acceptance
→ Business approval
→ Security approval
→ Legal approval
→ Procurement approval

↓

**7. Monitor**

→ Security posture
→ KRIs
→ Incidents
→ SLA performance
→ Regulatory changes
→ Certifications
→ Fourth parties
→ Material changes

↓

**8. Reassess**

→ Periodic reassessment
→ Trigger-based assessment
→ Contract renewal review

↓

**9. Remediate**

→ Findings
→ Corrective actions
→ Exceptions
→ Risk acceptance

↓

**10. Exit**

→ Revoke access
→ Return/delete data
→ Transition services

# Third-Party Risk Tiering Model

A practical GRC organization can classify vendors using a model such as:

| Tier       | Example Classification | Typical Treatment                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier 1** | Critical               | Enhanced due diligence, senior approval, contractual controls, annual assessment, continuous monitoring, resilience testing |
| **Tier 2** | High                   | Detailed assessment, security evidence, contractual controls, periodic monitoring                                           |
| **Tier 3** | Medium                 | Standard questionnaire and periodic reassessment                                                                            |
| **Tier 4** | Low                    | Simplified due diligence and basic monitoring                                                                               |

The exact thresholds should be defined by the organization's **risk appetite and regulatory environment** rather than copied blindly from another organization.

# Third-Party Risk vs. Supply Chain Risk

These concepts overlap but are not identical.

| Concept                  | Focus                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Third-Party Risk**     | Risk associated with a direct external relationship                      |
| **Fourth-Party Risk**    | Risk from the third party's own suppliers                                |
| **Supply Chain Risk**    | Broader risk across the interconnected supplier ecosystem                |
| **Vendor Risk**          | Risk specifically associated with a vendor                               |
| **ICT Third-Party Risk** | Risk associated with external technology providers                       |
| **Concentration Risk**   | Risk created by excessive dependence on common providers or dependencies |

### Key GRC takeaway

The central principle of third-party risk management is:

> **An organization may outsource a service, but it cannot automatically outsource accountability for the risks created by that service.**

Therefore, a mature TPRM program connects:

**Third-Party Identification → Risk Tiering → Due Diligence → Contractual Controls → Approval → Continuous Monitoring → Reassessment → Remediation → Exit**

This makes TPRM a critical component of the broader **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)** operating model.
