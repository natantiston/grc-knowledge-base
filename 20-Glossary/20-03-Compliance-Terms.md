# 20.3 Compliance Terms

This section defines the core terminology used in **regulatory compliance, legal compliance, cybersecurity compliance, privacy compliance, contractual compliance, standards compliance, and GRC compliance management**.

| Term                                           | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Compliance**                                 | The state of meeting applicable laws, regulations, standards, policies, contractual obligations, and other defined requirements.                                                                             |
| **Regulatory Compliance**                      | Conformance with requirements established by governmental or regulatory authorities.                                                                                                                         |
| **Legal Compliance**                           | Adherence to applicable laws, statutes, legal obligations, and legally enforceable requirements.                                                                                                             |
| **Contractual Compliance**                     | Fulfillment of obligations established through contracts, agreements, service-level agreements, or other binding arrangements.                                                                               |
| **Policy Compliance**                          | Adherence to internally established organizational policies and requirements.                                                                                                                                |
| **Standards Compliance**                       | Conformance with the requirements or criteria of an applicable standard, framework, or specification.                                                                                                        |
| **Compliance Management**                      | The coordinated processes used to identify, assess, implement, monitor, report, and maintain compliance with applicable requirements.                                                                        |
| **Compliance Management System (CMS)**         | A structured system of governance, policies, processes, controls, responsibilities, monitoring, and improvement activities used to manage compliance.                                                        |
| **Compliance Program**                         | An organized set of activities designed to establish, maintain, monitor, and demonstrate compliance with applicable requirements.                                                                            |
| **Compliance Framework**                       | A structured set of principles, requirements, processes, controls, and governance mechanisms used to manage compliance.                                                                                      |
| **Compliance Function**                        | The organizational function responsible for supporting, coordinating, monitoring, advising on, or overseeing compliance activities.                                                                          |
| **Compliance Officer**                         | A designated individual responsible for coordinating or overseeing compliance responsibilities within a defined scope.                                                                                       |
| **Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)**             | The senior executive responsible for leading or overseeing the organization's compliance function.                                                                                                           |
| **Compliance Owner**                           | The person or organizational unit accountable for achieving and maintaining compliance with a particular requirement or compliance domain.                                                                   |
| **Compliance Responsibility**                  | A formally assigned obligation to perform activities necessary to satisfy applicable compliance requirements.                                                                                                |
| **Compliance Obligation**                      | A requirement that an organization is legally, contractually, regulatorily, or internally required to fulfill.                                                                                               |
| **Compliance Requirement**                     | A specific obligation, rule, condition, or criterion that must be satisfied.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Applicable Requirement**                     | A requirement determined to apply to an organization, business unit, process, system, service, location, or activity.                                                                                        |
| **Mandatory Requirement**                      | A requirement that must be satisfied because it is legally, contractually, regulatorily, or organizationally binding.                                                                                        |
| **Voluntary Standard**                         | A standard that is not legally mandatory but may be adopted to improve governance, security, quality, or organizational performance.                                                                         |
| **Regulation**                                 | An authoritative rule or requirement issued by a government or regulatory authority.                                                                                                                         |
| **Regulatory Authority**                       | A governmental or officially authorized body responsible for establishing, supervising, or enforcing regulatory requirements.                                                                                |
| **Regulator**                                  | An authority responsible for monitoring and enforcing compliance with applicable laws or regulations.                                                                                                        |
| **Regulatory Requirement**                     | A specific requirement established by an applicable regulator or regulatory framework.                                                                                                                       |
| **Regulatory Obligation**                      | A legally or regulatorily binding responsibility that an organization must fulfill.                                                                                                                          |
| **Regulatory Change**                          | A modification, introduction, repeal, or reinterpretation of a law, regulation, regulatory requirement, or regulatory expectation.                                                                           |
| **Regulatory Change Management**               | The structured process for identifying, assessing, implementing, and monitoring changes to regulatory requirements.                                                                                          |
| **Regulatory Horizon Scanning**                | The systematic monitoring of proposed, emerging, or changing laws, regulations, standards, and regulatory expectations.                                                                                      |
| **Regulatory Interpretation**                  | The process of determining the meaning, applicability, and organizational implications of a regulatory requirement.                                                                                          |
| **Regulatory Applicability**                   | The determination of whether a particular law, regulation, rule, or regulatory requirement applies to an organization or activity.                                                                           |
| **Regulatory Scope**                           | The organizational, geographic, operational, technological, or legal boundaries within which a regulatory requirement applies.                                                                               |
| **Regulatory Mapping**                         | The process of mapping regulatory requirements to organizational processes, controls, policies, systems, and evidence.                                                                                       |
| **Regulatory Register**                        | A controlled inventory of applicable laws, regulations, regulatory requirements, authorities, obligations, and related information.                                                                          |
| **Compliance Register**                        | A centralized record of applicable compliance obligations, requirements, owners, controls, assessments, evidence, and status.                                                                                |
| **Obligation Register**                        | A structured inventory of legal, regulatory, contractual, and other obligations that an organization is required to fulfill.                                                                                 |
| **Requirement Register**                       | A controlled repository of applicable requirements and associated ownership, implementation, monitoring, and evidence information.                                                                           |
| **Compliance Obligation Inventory**            | A comprehensive collection of obligations applicable to an organization, function, process, or service.                                                                                                      |
| **Requirement Mapping**                        | The process of linking compliance requirements to policies, controls, processes, systems, risks, and evidence.                                                                                               |
| **Control Mapping**                            | The process of associating requirements with specific controls designed to satisfy those requirements.                                                                                                       |
| **Compliance Control**                         | A control implemented to prevent, detect, correct, or demonstrate compliance with a defined requirement.                                                                                                     |
| **Compliance Control Framework**               | A structured collection of controls designed to address applicable compliance obligations and requirements.                                                                                                  |
| **Control Objective**                          | A desired condition that a control or group of controls is intended to achieve.                                                                                                                              |
| **Compliance Evidence**                        | Documented or recorded information demonstrating that a compliance requirement or control has been implemented and/or operated as required.                                                                  |
| **Evidence Repository**                        | A controlled location where compliance and control evidence is collected, maintained, protected, and retrieved.                                                                                              |
| **Evidence Owner**                             | The person or function responsible for providing, maintaining, validating, or protecting required compliance evidence.                                                                                       |
| **Evidence Collection**                        | The process of gathering information necessary to demonstrate compliance or control effectiveness.                                                                                                           |
| **Evidence Retention**                         | The controlled preservation of compliance evidence for a defined period in accordance with applicable requirements.                                                                                          |
| **Evidence Sufficiency**                       | The degree to which evidence is adequate to support a compliance or assurance conclusion.                                                                                                                    |
| **Evidence Integrity**                         | The degree to which evidence remains accurate, complete, authentic, reliable, and protected from unauthorized alteration.                                                                                    |
| **Compliance Assessment**                      | A structured evaluation of whether defined compliance requirements are being satisfied.                                                                                                                      |
| **Compliance Review**                          | A formal examination of compliance status, activities, controls, evidence, or obligations.                                                                                                                   |
| **Compliance Testing**                         | The examination of controls, processes, records, or evidence to determine whether defined compliance requirements are being met.                                                                             |
| **Compliance Monitoring**                      | The ongoing or periodic observation of compliance status, controls, requirements, and changes.                                                                                                               |
| **Compliance Measurement**                     | The use of defined metrics or indicators to evaluate compliance performance.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Compliance Reporting**                       | The communication of compliance status, findings, risks, exceptions, and remediation activities to relevant stakeholders.                                                                                    |
| **Compliance Dashboard**                       | A consolidated visual representation of compliance status, requirements, findings, risks, exceptions, actions, and metrics.                                                                                  |
| **Compliance Metric**                          | A measurement used to evaluate the effectiveness, status, or performance of compliance activities.                                                                                                           |
| **Compliance Key Performance Indicator (KPI)** | A metric used to measure the performance or effectiveness of a compliance program or activity.                                                                                                               |
| **Compliance Key Risk Indicator (KRI)**        | A metric used to identify increasing exposure to compliance-related risk.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Compliance Status**                          | The current state of an organization's conformity with a defined requirement or set of requirements.                                                                                                         |
| **Compliant**                                  | A condition in which applicable requirements have been adequately satisfied based on defined assessment criteria.                                                                                            |
| **Noncompliance**                              | A failure to meet an applicable requirement.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Nonconformity**                              | Failure to fulfill a specified requirement, often used in management-system and standards contexts.                                                                                                          |
| **Major Nonconformity**                        | A significant failure or systemic deficiency that substantially affects the ability to meet an applicable requirement or achieve intended outcomes.                                                          |
| **Minor Nonconformity**                        | A limited or isolated failure to meet a requirement that does not substantially undermine the overall compliance system.                                                                                     |
| **Compliance Gap**                             | The difference between an applicable requirement and the organization's current state of implementation or conformity.                                                                                       |
| **Gap Analysis**                               | A structured comparison between current organizational practices and defined compliance requirements to identify deficiencies or improvement opportunities.                                                  |
| **Compliance Gap Assessment**                  | An assessment performed to determine the extent to which current practices satisfy applicable requirements.                                                                                                  |
| **Compliance Deficiency**                      | A weakness or shortcoming that prevents or may prevent full compliance with a requirement.                                                                                                                   |
| **Compliance Finding**                         | A documented result of an assessment, audit, review, or test identifying a condition requiring attention.                                                                                                    |
| **Compliance Issue**                           | A matter that may affect the organization's ability to meet an applicable requirement.                                                                                                                       |
| **Compliance Breach**                          | An actual violation or failure to satisfy a defined compliance requirement.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Regulatory Breach**                          | A violation of an applicable law, regulation, regulatory requirement, or regulatory order.                                                                                                                   |
| **Compliance Incident**                        | An event involving actual or potential failure to meet a compliance obligation or requirement.                                                                                                               |
| **Compliance Violation**                       | An act, omission, or condition that breaches an applicable requirement.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Compliance Exception**                       | A formally documented and authorized deviation from a compliance requirement or internal implementation standard where permitted.                                                                            |
| **Compliance Waiver**                          | Formal authorization allowing a defined requirement to be temporarily or conditionally excluded or modified, where such waiver is legally permissible.                                                       |
| **Compliance Exemption**                       | A formally recognized situation in which a requirement does not apply or an authorized exemption permits deviation from it.                                                                                  |
| **Compliance Tolerance**                       | The defined level of acceptable deviation from a compliance requirement or target, where such tolerance is permitted.                                                                                        |
| **Compliance Remediation**                     | Actions taken to correct a compliance deficiency, violation, gap, or control weakness.                                                                                                                       |
| **Remediation Plan**                           | A documented plan identifying corrective actions, owners, deadlines, priorities, and expected outcomes for addressing compliance deficiencies.                                                               |
| **Corrective Action**                          | An action taken to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity or deficiency and prevent recurrence.                                                                                                     |
| **Preventive Action**                          | An action taken to eliminate the cause of a potential nonconformity or undesirable event.                                                                                                                    |
| **Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)**    | A structured process for addressing existing deficiencies and preventing similar deficiencies from recurring.                                                                                                |
| **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)**                  | A structured process used to identify the underlying causes of a compliance failure, incident, or control deficiency.                                                                                        |
| **Compliance Action Plan**                     | A structured set of actions designed to address identified compliance gaps, findings, risks, or obligations.                                                                                                 |
| **Compliance Commitment**                      | A formally established commitment to satisfy a defined compliance requirement or remediation obligation.                                                                                                     |
| **Compliance Attestation**                     | A formal statement by an authorized individual confirming compliance status or the performance of specified controls or requirements.                                                                        |
| **Management Attestation**                     | A formal assertion by management regarding the accuracy, completeness, effectiveness, or compliance status of a defined area.                                                                                |
| **Certification**                              | A formal confirmation by an authorized party that specified requirements, standards, or criteria have been met.                                                                                              |
| **Accreditation**                              | Formal recognition by an authorized body that an organization is competent to perform specified activities or provide specified certification or assessment services.                                        |
| **Certification Body**                         | An organization authorized or accredited to assess and certify conformity with specified standards or requirements.                                                                                          |
| **Conformity Assessment**                      | The process of demonstrating whether specified requirements have been fulfilled.                                                                                                                             |
| **Declaration of Conformity**                  | A formal statement by an organization declaring that a product, service, system, or process meets specified requirements.                                                                                    |
| **Audit**                                      | A systematic, independent, and documented process for obtaining and evaluating evidence objectively to determine the extent to which defined criteria are fulfilled.                                         |
| **Compliance Audit**                           | An audit designed to evaluate conformity with applicable laws, regulations, standards, policies, contracts, or other requirements.                                                                           |
| **Regulatory Audit**                           | An audit conducted by or on behalf of a regulatory authority to assess compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.                                                                                   |
| **Internal Compliance Audit**                  | An organization-led audit performed to evaluate compliance with applicable internal and external requirements.                                                                                               |
| **External Audit**                             | An audit performed by an independent external organization or party.                                                                                                                                         |
| **Third-Party Audit**                          | An audit performed by an independent external party that is not the organization being audited.                                                                                                              |
| **Audit Criterion**                            | The policies, procedures, standards, regulations, contractual obligations, or other requirements against which audit evidence is evaluated.                                                                  |
| **Audit Evidence**                             | Records, statements, observations, data, or other information used to support audit conclusions.                                                                                                             |
| **Audit Finding**                              | A documented result of an audit indicating conformity, nonconformity, deficiency, or an opportunity for improvement.                                                                                         |
| **Audit Trail**                                | A chronological record showing activities, transactions, changes, approvals, or events that enables reconstruction and verification of actions.                                                              |
| **Compliance Audit Trail**                     | Evidence demonstrating how compliance activities, decisions, controls, approvals, and remediation actions were performed over time.                                                                          |
| **Regulatory Examination**                     | A formal review performed by a regulatory authority to determine whether an organization satisfies applicable regulatory requirements.                                                                       |
| **Regulatory Inspection**                      | An examination or investigation performed by a regulator or authorized authority.                                                                                                                            |
| **Regulatory Reporting**                       | The submission of required information, declarations, notifications, or reports to a regulatory authority.                                                                                                   |

| **Compliance Risk**                            | The risk of legal, regulatory, contractual, financial, operational, or reputational consequences resulting from failure to comply with applicable requirements.                                              |

| **Compliance Obligation Owner**                | The individual or function accountable for ensuring that a specific compliance obligation is fulfilled.                                                                                                      |
| **Regulatory Owner**                           | The person or function responsible for monitoring, interpreting, coordinating, or managing a defined regulatory requirement or regulatory domain.                                                            |
| **Regulatory Liaison**                         | A designated individual or function responsible for coordinating communications and interactions with regulatory authorities.                                                                                |
| **Regulatory Relationship Management**         | The structured management of interactions, communications, submissions, examinations, and relationships with regulators.                                                                                     |

| **Compliance Calendar**                        | A schedule of recurring compliance activities, assessments, certifications, filings, reviews, renewals, and reporting deadlines.                                                                             |

## Key Compliance Distinctions

### 1. Compliance vs. Conformity

**Compliance** generally refers to meeting applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, or organizational requirements.

**Conformity** generally refers to fulfillment of specified requirements, particularly within standards and management-system contexts.

For example:

> An organization may be **conforming to ISO 27001 requirements** while separately being **compliant with applicable privacy legislation**.

The terms may overlap, but they should not automatically be treated as identical.

---

### 2. Regulation vs. Standard vs. Policy

These represent different sources of requirements:

**Regulation**
→ Requirement established by a governmental or regulatory authority.

**Standard**
→ Defined set of criteria or best practices established by a recognized standards organization or industry body.

**Policy**
→ Internal organizational requirement approved by management or governance authority.

For example:

**NIS2** → Regulatory requirement
**ISO/IEC 27001** → International standard
**Information Security Policy** → Internal organizational requirement

---

### 3. Requirement vs. Control

A **requirement** states **what must be achieved or satisfied**.

A **control** describes **how the organization prevents, detects, corrects, or demonstrates compliance with that requirement**.

Example:

> **Requirement:** Access to sensitive systems must be appropriately controlled.
> **Control:** Privileged accounts must use MFA and undergo quarterly access reviews.
> **Evidence:** MFA configuration records and quarterly access-review reports.

---

### 4. Compliance Gap vs. Noncompliance

A **compliance gap** identifies a difference between the current state and a required state.

**Noncompliance** indicates that an applicable requirement has not been fulfilled.

A gap analysis can therefore identify potential or confirmed noncompliance, but not every gap necessarily represents a legal or regulatory violation. The significance depends on the applicable requirement and assessment criteria.

---

### 5. Audit vs. Compliance Assessment

A **compliance assessment** determines whether requirements are being satisfied.

An **audit** is a systematic, independent, and documented examination against defined audit criteria.

Therefore, an organization can perform compliance assessments as part of its broader compliance management activities, while audits provide a more formally structured assurance mechanism.

---

### 6. Exception vs. Exemption vs. Waiver

These terms should be carefully distinguished:

**Exception**
→ A formally approved deviation from an established requirement or control implementation.

**Exemption**
→ A situation where a requirement does not apply or an authorized exemption permits nonapplication.

**Waiver**
→ Formal authorization allowing a defined requirement to be temporarily or conditionally modified or not enforced, where legally permissible.

A waiver or exception **cannot override a mandatory legal requirement unless the applicable law or regulator explicitly permits such treatment**.

---

### 7. Compliance Evidence vs. Audit Evidence

**Compliance evidence** demonstrates that a compliance requirement or associated control has been implemented or satisfied.

**Audit evidence** is information obtained and evaluated by an auditor to support an audit conclusion.

Compliance evidence can therefore become audit evidence when it is appropriately relevant, reliable, sufficient, and evaluated within an audit.

---

## Compliance Management Lifecycle

A mature compliance program can be represented as:

**Identify → Determine Applicability → Interpret → Map → Implement → Collect Evidence → Assess → Monitor → Report → Remediate → Verify → Improve**

This lifecycle connects regulatory obligations to operational controls and ultimately to governance reporting.

A typical GRC traceability chain is:

**Law / Regulation**
↓
**Regulatory Requirement**
↓
**Compliance Obligation**
↓
**Risk**
↓
**Control Objective**
↓
**Control**
↓
**Evidence**
↓
**Testing / Assessment**
↓
**Finding**
↓
**Remediation**
↓
**Verification**
↓
**Compliance Reporting**

This traceability model is particularly important for demonstrating that an organization is not merely maintaining policies, but can actually **demonstrate how regulatory requirements are implemented, controlled, tested, evidenced, and governed**.
